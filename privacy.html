Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This policy covers the publishing tool used by the Facebook Page "Leonhadavision" (the "Page"), which posts chess content: daily puzzles, tips, news and tournament coverage.

What the tool does

The tool is a private automation owned by the Page administrator. It only publishes our own posts (images and videos) to our own Page using the Facebook Graph API. It does not read your account, your messages, your friends or your activity.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or sell personal data from visitors or users. The tool does not use cookies, trackers or analytics. The only credential it holds is the Page access token of the Page administrator, stored as an encrypted secret and used solely to publish posts.

Third parties

Posts are published through Facebook (Meta). Puzzle data comes from Lichess (CC0). Photos are credited to their authors. Meta's own privacy policy applies to how Facebook handles your data when you interact with the Page.

Data deletion

Because we do not collect personal data, there is nothing to delete. If you want a comment or message you sent to the Page removed, contact us at the email below, or delete it directly on Facebook.
